What is Carbonix

Quality is built at the moment of service

In today's beverage world, quality is defined not only by flavour but by the experience of the pour. Drawing on technical know-how, precise design and international standards, we build industrial carbonators and soda systems for beverages that come out cold, consistent and professional.

Our products combine engineering innovation, minimal design and precise in-house assembly. Every unit — from the cooling system to the serving tower — is built with detailed engineering and multi-stage quality control, for continuous, safe and dependable operation in any working environment.

Engineered technology

1 / 4 The QuickCarb System

Rapid Carbonation

Carbonation on site, on service time — not on warehouse time.

The traditional route leaves a beverage sitting cold for hours to absorb gas. QuickCarb intensifies gas-to-liquid contact inside the vessel through controlled CO₂ and nitrogen injection, so the drink is ready within the same shift — with even, repeatable carbonation.

2 / 4 Reservoir + serve line

Dual-Stage Cooling

Chilled twice so it pours right once.

Stage one chills the beverage reservoirs inside the unit. Stage two chills the liquid again as it travels through the steel coil on its way to the tap. The result is a stable serving temperature during peak hours — exactly where single-stage systems tend to drift.

3 / 4 CO₂ + N₂

Multi-Gas Ready

One platform, many drink styles.

Carbonix runs on CO₂, nitrogen, or a blend of the two. That single capability stretches the menu from sparkling water and craft soda to nitro cold brew and creamy pours — without buying a second machine.

4 / 4 1–6 outlets

Custom Towers

Half equipment, half architecture.

Outlet count, tower geometry, colour and finish — polished or brushed steel, copper, custom powder coat — are all specified by you. Towers are fabricated in our own workshop, so your counter geometry and brand identity drive the design, not a foreign supplier's catalogue.

How it works

  1. Liquid enters the Cornelius keg

  2. It meets CO₂ or nitrogen directly

  3. QuickCarb accelerates carbonation

  4. The liquid is chilled in two stages

  5. It is served through the tower and taps
